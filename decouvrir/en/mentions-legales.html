User reviews

The reviews published on the home page are real reviews, collected by Requête from people who use the app and published with their consent, from newest to oldest.

The audio reviews at the top of the home page are read by a synthetic voice generated with ElevenLabs from the text of these reviews: it is not the voice of the people themselves. The text read is that of their written review, published on the same page. The same audio reviews appear in the app, on the “They love Requête” screen of the welcome flow, read by the same synthetic voice.

Lesson voices

The voice that reads the app’s video lessons is a synthetic voice, generated with ElevenLabs from the text of the lessons, which the video also shows as captions.
