Purpose

These terms govern the use of the Requête website and app, an app for learning SQL by practicing. By using Requête, you accept them. The publisher is presented in the legal notice.

Access to the service

Requête is an iPhone app, available on the App Store; this website presents it. To use it, you choose a Requête Premium subscription (see below); signing in with Apple or Google is optional and saves your progress to your account. You need an internet connection to subscribe and to sign in; after that, lessons also work offline.

The publisher may change the service, add or modify lessons, and occasionally interrupt it for maintenance.

Learning content

The lessons, exercises, case studies and interview questions are provided for learning purposes, as is. Requête teaches you SQL and trains you, but does not guarantee a job, an interview or a salary level, and does not award any certification or diploma.

What you can and can’t do

  • Use Requête to learn, for personal purposes.
  • Don’t copy, resell or republish the lessons, videos or exercises.
  • Don’t disrupt the service or try to access it other than through its screens.
  • Choose a respectful username and reactions, wherever they appear (challenge rooms, leaderboard).

Zero tolerance. No abusive, hateful, harassing, sexual or discriminatory username, reaction or behavior is tolerated. Every player can report and block another player (“Report and block”, on their profile or in a room). We review every report within 24 hours: a username that breaks these rules is removed, and the player who chose it may be excluded from the service, without notice.

Your data and your files

Your progress is stored on your iPhone and, if you sign in (it’s optional), saved to your account. The files you import stay on your iPhone, and remain yours. You can download a copy of your data at any time (My account, “Download my data”). You can also delete your account from the app (My account, “Delete my account”). The details are in the privacy policy.

Requête Premium subscription

Access to the lessons and practice requires a Requête Premium subscription, sold in the app through the App Store. Before any purchase, the app shows the price, the subscription length and its renewal terms.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, from My account › “Manage my subscription” or your Apple ID settings.
  • If a free trial is offered, it becomes paid when it ends, unless canceled before the end of the trial.
  • Deleting your Requête account does not cancel the subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, under its terms.
  • The App Store terms also apply to the purchase.

Liability

The publisher does its best to keep Requête working and its content accurate, but cannot guarantee it is free of errors or interruptions. Report any error to us through the contact form or in the app (My account, “Contact us”): we’ll fix it.

Changes to these terms

These terms may change along with the service. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of the page.

Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by French law. If there is a disagreement, write to us first: we’ll look for an amicable solution. If you are a consumer and your written complaint has not received a satisfactory answer within two months, you can refer the matter free of charge to the consumer mediator, within one year of that complaint (articles L. 611-1 and R. 612-1 et seq. of the French Consumer Code): MCP Médiation, online at www.mcpmediation.org, or by mail to Médiation de la Consommation et Patrimoine, 12 square Desnouettes, 75015 Paris.
