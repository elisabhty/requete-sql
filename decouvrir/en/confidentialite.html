Privacy Policy

Requête is an iPhone app: you can sign in with Apple or Google (it’s optional), and your account then saves your progress. This website presents it. Here is exactly what moves around, and why.

Data controller

The data controller is the publisher of Requête (Elisa Blanchart, sole proprietor, trade name HORMEND AI LABS), reachable at contact@requeteapp.com.

What stays on your device

Your first name, age range, gender and occupation (if you give them), your country (only its flag appears next to your username in challenges, see below), your list of challenge friends, how you heard about Requête, your goal, your level, your reminder time, your progress, your notes, your schedule, your streaks, your cards and the databases you import are stored in the app, on your iPhone. Part of this is also saved to your account (see “Your account”); what leaves your iPhone is detailed below.

The files you import (Excel, CSV, TSV, JSON, SQLite) are read and turned into tables right on your iPhone: they never leave your device.

You can download a copy or erase them from My account, in the app. Erasing is permanent if you haven’t downloaded a copy first.

Your account

In the app, you can sign in with Apple or Google, with no password on our side. It’s optional: if you don’t sign in, everything stays on your device. Your account is held by the app’s database (Supabase, Paris).

  • What we receive when you sign in. An account identifier and the email address provided by Apple or Google (with Apple, you can choose to hide your address: we then receive a relay address). Your first name, when Apple or Google provides it, to greet you in the app. With Google, the profile information it attaches to the sign-in (your name and the address of your profile picture) is also stored with the account; we don’t use it.
  • What is saved. Your completed lessons and exercises, challenges won, answers to interview questions, active days (your streak), your schedule and your notes (text and queries). They are sent a few seconds after each change, so you can find them on another device or after reinstalling. For challenges: the player identifier randomly generated by your device (the one that carries your username and points on the leaderboard), so your username and points follow you, and your game stats (points, rounds, places, and the usernames of players in your games with friends), with your last 500 games.
  • What stays on the device. The databases and files you import, and your settings.
  • With Apple. To be able to revoke “Sign in with Apple” access if you delete your account, a token provided by Apple is kept on the server side, never in the app.

What it’s for: saving your progress and giving it back to you. None of this is used for advertising. Signing out (My account) leaves your progress on your account.

What leaves your device

  • Website hosting. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States): when you open it, its servers receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type, notably for the security of the service. The app also reads from it, at most twice a day, the list of its reminder messages: a simple file, for which GitHub receives the same technical data. Finally, when you open the app (at most once every 30 minutes), it checks there whether a corrected version of its lessons and screens has been published, and then downloads only the files that changed: here too, GitHub receives this technical data (your IP address, your iOS version). See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • SQL engine. It is included in the app and its updates: it is not downloaded from any other service.
  • Challenge rooms (optional). A room only shares your username, your flag, your times and your reactions with the other players in the room. A public room (the option suggested when you create one) appears in the list of open rooms (“Join a game”), with its players’ usernames and flags, its level and its free spots, while it is waiting for players; an invite-only room is visible only to those who have its code. These messages go through the app’s database, hosted by Supabase in Paris (European Union), which also receives your IP address; they are deleted after 24 hours. For games checked online, your SQL query is sent to Supabase’s verification service, in the Paris region, then run on an isolated copy of the practice database. It is not shared with other players or stored in plain text in the app’s database: only its fingerprint, with the check result and the time, is kept for at most 24 hours. The queries in your personal console and your imported files stay on your device. See Supabase’s privacy policy.
  • Your username. It is unique in the app: when you choose it (right after signing up), it is reserved in the same database (Supabase, Paris) with an identifier randomly generated by your device, not your name or your email. Your country is stored with it, to show your flag next to your username in challenges; you can hide it in My account › Country (“Show my flag”). Your level in the path (from 1 to 5, based on your completed lessons) is also stored there, so other players can see it on your player profile. When you add a friend by their username, that username is only checked in the database. Your username can’t be changed afterwards, and it stays yours; “Delete my account” frees it along with your scores (see below). Your first name stays on your device.
  • Overall challenge leaderboard. At the end of each game, your username, your points and your number of games are sent to the same database (Supabase, Paris), with the same identifier: not your name or your email. The list of challenges you have already played and solved goes with it (never your queries): it is shown to no one and only helps us stop drawing them for you, and for those playing with you, and know when to add new challenges. Other players see the leaderboard (5 players per page, with their flag) for everyone, a country or their friends, all-time or since Monday, and their own place. Usernames are filtered, and every player can report one: a username reported several times is hidden and then checked (the reported username is emailed to us, through the Resend email service, so we can check it quickly). This data stays as long as you play; “Reset progress” and “Delete my account” erase it.
  • Lesson ratings (optional). If you rate a lesson at the end, your rating (1 to 5 stars), your comment if you leave one, the lesson’s number and title and the date are stored in the app’s database (Supabase, Paris), with the identifier randomly generated by your device: not your name or your email. One rating per lesson, which you can change. When it includes a comment, it is also emailed to us (the rating, the comment, the lesson and the date, without your identifier) through the Resend email service (United States). They only help us improve the lessons; to erase them, write to us from the app (My account, “Contact us”).
  • Welcome screens (optional, with your consent). If you accept anonymous usage statistics (see just below), the app’s welcome screens you saw (the welcome questions) are stored in the app’s database (Supabase, Paris): their name and the date, with the identifier randomly generated by your device, not your name, your email or your answers. We only derive a count from them (how many devices saw each screen), to find where the welcome flow is too long. The same count continues after the welcome flow: the first lesson started, the first and second lessons completed, and the times the app asks iOS to offer you to rate it (your rating itself never reaches Requête). Without your consent, none of this leaves your device. To erase them, write to us from the app (My account, “Contact us”).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (optional, with your consent). The app asks you once, right after “Start”: “Will you help us improve Requête?”, with two answers, “Yes, happy to” and “No thanks”. Until you say yes, nothing is sent. If you say yes, to understand how the app is used and improve it, it sends short events to the same database (Supabase, Paris): the day you open it (with the platform and version), flames lit and streaks broken, lessons started and completed, videos left before the end, “Your turn” moments passed or not, challenges and games played, rooms created or joined (through an invite link or by code), invites shared and invite links opened (without the room code), rooms left (while waiting, during a game or at the end), reviews, quizzes and interview questions (passed or not, never your answer), hints requested, time spent in the app, the days you use the console and the number of tables you import (never their content), stickers earned, cards and flames shared, shortcuts tapped, the paywall seen (and the screen it came from) and the offer chosen, reminders (turned on, tapped), the widget and, once a day, the widgets placed (their kind and size), and the app’s error messages (with the place in the code or the server address they come from). They are linked to an identifier scrambled twice from your device’s: it doesn’t lead to your name, your email or your username. Never your queries or your answers. You can give or withdraw your consent at any time in My account › Your data, your choices; if you withdraw it, what has already been sent is erased.
  • Contact messages. From the app (My account, “Contact us”), your message, its subject (a bug, an idea, a question…), the screenshots or files you attach (3 at most, 5 MB each, in private storage), your email address if you give it and, for a bug, technical info you can uncheck (app version, device and browser type, screen size, language) are stored in the app’s database (Supabase, Paris), with the identifier randomly generated by your device. So we can read it right away, a copy (your message, its subject, your email address if you gave it and this technical info) is emailed to us through the Resend email service (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States); your files aren’t attached, only a viewing link valid for 7 days. The website’s form prepares an email in your email app: if you send it, we receive your first name, your email address, your message and the files you attach (screenshots, documents). These messages are only used to reply to you and fix the app; we keep them while handling your request, then for at most 3 years after our last exchange.
  • Daily reminder. It only turns on if you allow notifications on your iPhone, and the notification is scheduled on your device.

Requête Premium subscription

The Requête Premium subscription is bought in the app, and payment goes through Apple’s App Store: we never receive your bank details. To know whether your subscription is active, the app relies on RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), an in-app purchase management provider. It receives an anonymous purchase identifier created on your iPhone, the purchase information provided by Apple (the offer chosen, the dates, the renewal status) and technical data such as your IP address, the app version and the iOS version: not your name, your email or your Requête account. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Deleting your Requête account does not cancel the subscription: it is managed from My account › “Manage my subscription”, or in your Apple ID settings.

Delete your account

In the app, My account › “Delete my account” (“Delete my data” if you haven’t signed in) erases at once, after two confirmations, everything the app’s database keeps with your device’s identifier: your username and country, your leaderboard place and games, your lesson ratings, the welcome screens seen, your contact messages, your places in rooms and your usage statistics. Your username becomes free again. If you are signed in, your account (identifier, email address, first name) and all saved progress are erased too, and “Sign in with Apple” access is revoked with Apple. The app then erases everything it keeps on your device (lessons, challenges, notes, schedule, projects) and starts over. Files attached to your messages, stored separately, are erased by us within the following month. If you no longer have the app, write to us with your username: we will delete it along with your scores.

How long we keep your data

  • Anonymous usage statistics: automatically erased after 13 months.
  • What stays on your device: as long as you keep it. “Reset progress” empties it, and deleting the app erases it too.
  • Challenge rooms (your username, your times, your reactions, your messages and the fingerprint of your queries): automatically erased after 24 hours, reactions after 10 minutes.
  • Contact messages: automatically erased 3 years after they were sent.
  • Your account and saved progress: as long as you keep your account. “Delete my account” erases them right away.
  • Your username, country, leaderboard place and games, ratings and welcome screens seen: as long as you keep your account. “Delete my account” erases them right away (see above); “Reset progress” already removes your scores from the leaderboard.

Cookies and trackers

Requête uses no advertising cookies, no third-party analytics tool and no tracking pixel. The only measurement is the one Requête does itself, and only if you accept it: the welcome screens and anonymous usage statistics (see above), asked once, at first launch. This website sets no cookies. In the app, the storage used makes the service you ask for work (keeping your progress and lessons on your iPhone): it is exempt from consent.

Sharing and selling

Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising. Apart from the services described above, it is shared with no one.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction, its erasure, the restriction of its processing, object to its processing or request its portability, by writing to contact@requeteapp.com or from the app (My account, “Contact us”); “Delete my account” erases everything at once. What never leaves your iPhone (your imported databases and files, your settings), we can neither view nor delete for you: you’re the one keeping it.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Changes to this policy

If what Requête stores changes (new data saved, a new service), this page will be updated beforehand, and its date at the top of the page will say so.
