Question 6 of 142Beginner · Filters

              
What’s the difference between WHERE and HAVING?

              
Answer out loud, then compare.

              

                
Your turn to answerTake a few seconds to structure your answer before looking.Reveal the answer

                

                  Expected answer
                  
WHERE filters rows before grouping; HAVING filters groups, after the GROUP BY.

                  
So WHERE doesn’t know about totals or counts like COUNT(*) yet. A condition on a column goes in WHERE; a condition on a total or a count goes in HAVING.

Questions and answers

      
Answers to the most common questions before you start.

      

        
Do I need programming knowledge to start?
No. The first lessons explain what databases do and take you all the way to your first SQL queries. When you start, you set your goal, your level and the pace you want, to personalize your path.

        
How much time should I plan to make progress?
You choose 10, 20 or 30 minutes per session. The schedule then organizes the activities to do at the pace you picked.

        
Do I need to install anything?
Yes. Requête is an iPhone app, available on the App Store. It runs SQL on a real SQLite database, right on your iPhone. Once you’re signed in, lessons also work offline; saving your progress and challenges with friends need an internet connection.

        
Which SQL topics are covered?
The path includes 71 lessons, from SELECT queries and their filters to joins, aggregates, subqueries, CTEs and window functions. It also covers creating and changing tables, constraints, transactions, views, indexes and triggers.

        
Can I practice with my own data files?
Yes. You can import Excel, CSV, JSON, SQL or SQLite files. The sheets of an Excel workbook become tables you can query in the SQL console.

        
Do I need an account to use Requête?
Yes. You sign in with one tap with Apple or Google, no password to create. Your progress is then saved to your account.

        
Where are my progress and notes stored?
On your iPhone and in your account: your completed lessons, challenges won, schedule and notes are saved there, so you can find them on another iPhone or after reinstalling. The files you import stay on your iPhone.

        
Do my files and SQL queries stay private?
Your imported files and console queries stay on your device. In games checked online, your queries are sent to Supabase in Paris to check the result, without being stored in plain text or shown to other players. Only your username, your times and your reactions are shared with them.

        
Can I use Requête on my phone?
Yes, on iPhone: Requête is a full-fledged app, available on the App Store. The Android version is coming soon to Google Play.

        
What does Requête offer after the lessons?
You can solve 477 case studies across fifteen business databases (Airport, Hospital, Chinese Wok, Cheese Shop, Travel Agency, Laboratory, Zoo, Gym, Ski Resort, Food Truck, Aquarium, Bike Share, Bee Farm, Escape Game and Music Festival), review concepts with spaced repetition, use the SQL console and its step-by-step visualizer, and challenge your friends.

        
Does the path prepare me for SQL interviews?
Yes. The app offers 142 SQL interview questions across three levels, from beginner to advanced, on topics like joins, aggregations, window functions, transactions and performance. You answer out loud, then compare with an answer and an example when useful. You can then mark whether you’ve got the concept or need to review it.
